Geospatial and astronomy applications running on remote compute and streamed to your browser — useful when the data is larger than a laptop wants to hold. Each opens maximized and keeps its databases under `/volume`.

This category covers:

- **QGIS** — desktop GIS for vector, raster, and map layouts
- **GRASS GIS** — geospatial processing, modelling, and temporal analysis
- **Stellarium** — a planetarium for any date and place on Earth

Browse the workflows below. To deploy one, see [How it works](/hub/getting-started/how).
