Design and drafting applications running on remote compute and streamed to your browser. Each opens maximized and renders through software OpenGL, since no runner carries a graphics card.

This category covers:

- **FreeCAD** — parametric 3D modelling, assemblies, and FEM
- **KiCad** — schematic capture, PCB layout, and 3D board review
- **LibreCAD** — 2D drafting and DXF work

Browse the workflows below. To deploy one, see [How it works](/hub/getting-started/how).
