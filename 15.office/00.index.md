Document and publishing applications running on remote compute and streamed to your browser. Each builds on the [Void Desktop](/hub/desktop/void) image, opens maximized, and keeps its files under `/volume`.

This category covers:

- **LibreOffice** — documents, spreadsheets, presentations, drawings, and databases
- **LyX** — structured writing on a LaTeX backend
- **Sigil** — EPUB ebook editing with a live preview

Browse the workflows below. To deploy one, see [How it works](/hub/getting-started/how).
